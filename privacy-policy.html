Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 5, 2026

Important: Interactive Presentations is an unofficial third-party browser extension. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with slides.com, Google Slides, or Google LLC.

1. Overview

Interactive Presentations ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension that allows presenters to add interactive polls, quizzes, and games to their presentations. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide the interactive presentation features:

2.1 Presenter Data

  • Session Information: Temporary session codes, presentation IDs, and session timing
  • Activity Configuration: Quiz questions, poll options, and game settings you create
  • Extension Settings: Stored locally in your browser only

2.2 Attendee Data

  • Display Name: The name you choose to display when joining a session
  • Responses: Your answers to polls, quizzes, and games
  • Session Activity: Join time, response timestamps, and participation status
  • Scores and Rankings: Points earned and leaderboard positions (if applicable)

2.3 Technical Data

  • Browser Storage: Session codes and participant IDs stored temporarily in browser storage
  • Firebase Connection: Real-time database connections for live updates

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Email addresses or contact information
  • Personal identifiers beyond display names you choose
  • Browsing history or activity outside of active sessions
  • Payment or financial information
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Content of your presentations (slides content is never accessed or stored)

4. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data exclusively to:

  • Enable real-time interactive features during presentation sessions
  • Display participant responses and aggregated results to presenters
  • Calculate scores and maintain leaderboards for games
  • Maintain session connections between presenters and attendees

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Where Data is Stored

All session data is stored in:

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database: Cloud-hosted database for real-time updates
  • Browser Local Storage: Temporary session information stored locally on your device

5.2 Security Measures

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Firebase security rules prevent unauthorized access
  • Session codes are randomly generated to prevent guessing
  • No authentication system means no password vulnerabilities

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Automatic Deletion: All session data is automatically and permanently deleted when the presenter ends the session.

6.1 Automatic Deletion

  • Session End: All participant names, responses, scores, and session data are immediately deleted from Firebase when the presenter clicks "End Session"
  • Browser Data: Local browser storage is cleared when you close the attendee app tab
  • No Long-term Storage: We do not retain any data after sessions end

6.2 Session Expiration

  • Sessions automatically expire 24 hours after creation if not ended manually
  • Expired sessions and all associated data are deleted

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

7.1 Visible Within Sessions

  • Your chosen display name is visible to the presenter and other participants during active sessions
  • Your responses and scores are visible to the presenter
  • Aggregated response statistics (e.g., "60% chose option A") are shown to presenters

7.2 Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Used for real-time database hosting. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy
  • QR Code API: QR codes are generated via a public API for easy session joining

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 As an Attendee

  • Choose Your Name: You control what display name to use
  • Leave Anytime: Close the browser tab to disconnect from a session
  • Data Deletion: All your data is deleted when the session ends

8.2 As a Presenter

  • End Sessions: Clicking "End Session" immediately deletes all session data
  • Uninstall Extension: Removes all locally stored preferences

9. Children's Privacy

This extension may be used in educational settings with students of all ages. We design our data collection to be minimal and appropriate for all users. No accounts are required, and students only provide a display name of their choosing. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by not collecting personal information from children.

10. Browser Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions:

  • activeTab: To detect when you're on a presentation page
  • storage: To save session codes and settings locally
  • scripting: To add interactive features to presentation pages
  • alarms: To maintain real-time connections during sessions
  • Host Permissions: Access to slides.com and/or Google Slides to integrate features

We do not access or collect data from any other websites or your browsing activity.
